# Public correction notice

The authoritative detailed record is
`PUBLICATION_AUDIT_I/CORRECTIONS.md`. The essential corrections are:

1. The bounded fixed-point result concerns finite bounded graph augmentations
   (or norms dominated by `L2`). It does not exclude source-selected
   unbounded operators or all singular extensions.
2. `RIGGING.TRADEOFF.NOGO` is not one theorem. The point-evaluation threshold,
   covariance, and rank-one restriction are known analytic results; order
   nonselection and missing arithmetic orbit data are scoped architectural
   observations.
3. Full multiplicative-group unitarity must be distinguished from unitarity
   of the p-adic unit subgroup.
4. The factor `p^{-m/2}` appears as covariance of point evaluation; it is not
   generated as the coefficient of the one-point Green form.
5. The proposed obstruction paper is viable only as an expository note, not
   as an original specialist research paper on the present theorem set.

These corrections supersede stronger historical wording.
