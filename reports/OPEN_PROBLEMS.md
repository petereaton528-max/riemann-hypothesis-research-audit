# Narrow open problems

These are concrete questions below RH, not proposed RH proof programmes.

## Mathematically well posed

1. **CCM–Meyer comparison:** construct, or rule out in a precise category, a
   functorial comparison that preserves radicals, multiplicities, and Jordan
   jets. Difficulty: HIGH.
2. **Continuous Xi-kernel PF3/PF4:** decide finite-order total positivity for
   the canonical continuous kernel, with certified analytic estimates rather
   than floating-point evidence. Difficulty: VERY HIGH.
3. **Discrete coefficient hierarchy:** identify the first genuinely
   unresolved full Toeplitz-minor order after separating it from consecutive
   minors and Jensen-polynomial hyperbolicity. Difficulty: HIGH.

## Verification and research debt

4. Independently reproduce the reported 2026 continuous-kernel PF5
   counterminor and its theta-tail bounds.
5. Re-audit `TAIL.COERCIVITY`, including domains, uniform finite-place bounds,
   and its separation from numerical thresholds.
6. Reproduce any retained computer-assisted Schur estimates with a second
   interval-arithmetic implementation before treating them as certified.

The former fixed-point and rigging audit problems are resolved by Publication
Audit I and are not open research targets.
