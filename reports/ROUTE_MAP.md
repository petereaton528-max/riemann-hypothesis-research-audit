# Public route-map summary

The detailed table is `PROJECT_CONSOLIDATION/MASTER_ROUTE_MAP.md`. Its status
terms are scoped:

- **OPEN:** a precise problem remains unresolved.
- **PARTIAL:** a real construction exists but lacks a required component.
- **CLOSED IN THE AUDITED NATURAL CLASS:** the stated assumptions lead to a
  proved obstruction; stronger or different source data are not ruled out.
- **RH-EQUIVALENT:** the required positivity or reality statement is another
  form of an RH criterion rather than an independent forcing theorem.
- **KNOWN REFORMULATION:** useful structure, but established in the
  literature or a re-expression of known theory.

At consolidation, 35 major routes were classified: 17 closed in their audited
natural classes, 6 RH-equivalent, 5 partial, 3 open, and 4 known
reformulations. Publication Audit I subsequently narrowed the Phase-22/23
language: the bounded fixed-point result is an elementary scoped corollary,
and the Vladimirov rigging label is not one theorem.

The five most consequential route conclusions are:

1. genuine zero-bearing realization does not by itself force the critical
   line;
2. direct critical Hilbert completion of the Meyer source collapses the
   fixed quotient in the audited model;
3. global positivity strong enough to force the line returns to Weil
   positivity/RH;
4. bounded Hilbert graph norms erase pointwise arithmetic fixed-point data,
   while the standard unbounded repair uses known point-interaction theory and
   still lacks the orbit/Gamma character;
5. natural finite-prime recursion, canonical interlacing, and strong total
   positivity did not yield an independent non-spectral route.
