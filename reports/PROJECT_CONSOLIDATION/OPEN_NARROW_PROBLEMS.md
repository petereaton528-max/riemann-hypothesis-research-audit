# Open narrow problems

These are research questions, not authorization to resume RH exploration.

## C1. Mathematically well-posed

### O1. CCM–Meyer comparison on generalized primary spaces — HIGH

Construct, or prove absent in a specified category, a source-defined functor
between the CCM cyclic cokernel and Meyer's nuclear quotient that identifies
the scaling character, trace-pairing radical, and every Jordan jet.

- Below RH: it asks for comparison of two unconditional carriers.
- Evidence: characters and zero divisors agree; no positive/topological
  isomorphism is supplied by the sources.
- Files: `research/phase25/CARRIER_LOCK.md`,
  `research/phase25/WEIL_INTERFACE.md`, `research/PHASE25_RESULT.md`.
- Tools: bornological/nuclear representation theory, cyclic homology,
  topological exact sequences.

### O2. Continuous Xi-kernel PF3/PF4 — VERY HIGH

Decide whether the translation kernel (K(x,y)=\Phi(x-y)) is PF3 or PF4.

- Below RH: fixed finite-order total positivity cannot force every Xi zero
  real.
- Evidence: PF2 is published; a 2026 preprint reports a certified PF5
  counterminor and leaves PF4 open.
- Files: `research/reset2/TOTAL_POSITIVITY.md`,
  `reports/PROJECT_CONSOLIDATION/PF_HIERARCHY_DISAMBIGUATION.md`.
- Tools: theta-tail bounds, determinant identities, interval certification,
  Karlin/Schoenberg theory.

### O3. First unresolved discrete coefficient PF order — HIGH

For the normalized positive coefficients (a_k) of
(G(x)=\Xi(\sqrt{-x})), determine global PF3 (or exhibit a counterminor),
with special attention to small shift/transition configurations.

- Below RH: PF3 is a fixed finite-order condition; PF-infinity is RH.
- Evidence: Turan/order-2 inequalities and strong fixed-order tail results;
  the (k=O(r)) region is not controlled by the 2026 cubic wedge.
- Files: `research/reset2/JENSEN_AUDIT.md`,
  `research/reset2/MOMENT_RIGIDITY.md`.
- Tools: theta moments, compound matrices, saddle estimates, exact/interval
  determinant bounds.

### O4. Specific CCM ground-state compact counting — VERY HIGH

Prove or disprove (N_\lambda(R)=O_R(1)) for the projectively normalized
transform of the *specific* CCM Weil-matrix ground state, under the relevant
simplicity hypothesis.

- Below RH: it is a compact normal-family/selection estimate, not a line
  theorem.
- Evidence: it kills the sine countermodel, but no source mechanism is known.
- Files: `proof_attempts/pre_phase13_onepoint_lemma.md`,
  `reports/PROJECT_RESET_AFTER_CCM_AUDIT/07_IDENTIFIABILITY_DEFICIT.md`.
- Tools: precise matrix ground-state estimates, Paley–Wiener zero counting.
- Policy: preserve only; do not attack absent new CCM input.
