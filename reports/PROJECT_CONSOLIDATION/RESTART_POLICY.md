# Restart policy

Broad RH exploration remains paused. A route may be reopened only if at least
one of the following is documented:

1. a new theorem or primary paper changes a stated obstruction;
2. a genuinely new source-derived object is constructed before its zero set;
3. one of the narrow open problems is solved or materially sharpened;
4. an external mature mechanism supplies a bridge not covered by the route
   map and passes the Riemann Trace Fingerprint;
5. a publication audit, rather than an RH goal, requires a scoped recheck.

Before reopening, the proposal must state:

- which exact row of `MASTER_ROUTE_MAP.md` changes;
- which closure hypothesis in `CLOSED_ROUTES.md` is violated;
- whether the new claim is local, finite-S, global distributional, or global
  positive;
- whether it uses zero data, RH-equivalent positivity, or a free regularity
  parameter;
- the first theorem strictly below RH and its falsification test.
