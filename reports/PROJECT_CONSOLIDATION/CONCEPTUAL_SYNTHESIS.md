# What changed our understanding

Before the project, the guiding intuition was diffuse: the explicit formula
looked trace-like, so perhaps the zeros should be eigenvalues of an arithmetic
self-adjoint operator. After Phase 26, several separations are exact.

1. **Realization is not line forcing.** Meyer and CCM already give genuine
   global zero-bearing carriers with multiplicity and the complete explicit
   formula. Their existence does not provide a positive polarization.
2. **Local fingerprint is not a global reality theorem.** Tate/Connes local
   fixed-point traces explain \((\log p)p^{-m/2}\), both iterate directions,
   and the Gamma sector. Assembly of these distributions still does not make
   the zero generator self-adjoint.
3. **Hilbert bulk and arithmetic fixed points are different data.**
   Half-density dilation is unitary off the fixed point, while the arithmetic
   flat trace is concentrated exactly at the measure-zero point discarded by
   Hilbert equivalence.
4. **Canonicity and regularity conflict.** Natural bounded norms erase point
   evaluation. Sobolev/Vladimirov norms recover it only above a threshold,
   with a free order and without the orbit character.
5. **Known positivity returns to RH.** The global CCM/Weil test form is
   positive exactly at RH strength; a faithful positive jet form would also
   force semisimplicity.
6. **Prime recursion is not an entire approximation mechanism.** Exact Euler
   insertion is naturally meromorphic, and one fixed infinity factor cannot
   absorb its growing common pole order.
7. **Total positivity has limited reach.** Continuous kernel PF2 is real but
   weak; PF-infinity is overstrong and false. Discrete Jensen/coefficient
   positivity has useful finite-order tails, but all-order positivity is RH.

The resulting picture is not that spectral or zero-confinement ideas are
wrong. It is that the source arithmetic, global carrier, and independent
reality mechanism are three distinct requirements, and the project found no
theorem joining all three.
