# Obstruction-paper assessment

## Proposed title

**Fixed-point traces versus Hilbert boundary data in adelic spectral models**

## One-sentence thesis

For the natural local half-density scaling source, arithmetic fixed-point
evaluation is invisible to bounded Hilbert graph norms, while the standard
unbounded (p)-adic Sobolev repair recovers a point defect only by introducing
a noncanonical regularity order and still fails to generate the arithmetic
orbit character.

## Audited mathematical set

1. The elementary bounded-graph invisibility lemma and its p-adic corollary.
2. The known point-evaluation threshold (s>1/2) in the normalized (p)-adic
   scale.
3. Scaling covariance, with full-group unitarity only at (s=0) and
   unit-subgroup unitarity at every order.
4. The known rank-one point restriction for (s>1/2).
5. The scoped observation that its Green form itself contains neither
   \(\log p\) nor the iterate support \(\pm m\log p\).

Dependency:
\[
\text{shrinking-ball lemma}
\to\text{bounded graph no-go},
\qquad
\text{shell integral}+\text{scaling covariance}
\to\text{rigging tradeoff}.
\]
The arithmetic-incompleteness conclusion then compares these local theorems
with the standard fixed-point trace formula.

## New versus background

- Elementary new corollary: the explicit bounded-graph application to the
  arithmetic flat-trace problem.
- Background: Vladimirov operators, point-interaction extension theory,
  normalized half-density scaling, Connes local fixed-point coefficients.
- Not claimed: impossibility of all riggings, all boundary relations, or all
  arithmetic spectral carriers.
