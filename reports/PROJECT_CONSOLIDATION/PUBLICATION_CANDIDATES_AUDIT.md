# Independent publication-candidate audit

## Candidate decisions

### FIXEDPOINT.GRAPH.NOGO — EXPOSITORY LEMMA ONLY

1. **Statement:** point evaluation at zero is discontinuous for every finite
   bounded graph augmentation of the local half-density scaling/Fourier
   algebra; the evaluation-zero restriction closes to the full bounded
   operator and creates no deficiency boundary.
2. **Proof:** self-contained and short.
3. **Novelty:** elementary new corollary of bounded graph-norm equivalence.
4. **Significance:** conceptually useful, but low technical depth and low
   standalone novelty.
5. **Grouping:** explanatory lemma in an expository fixed-point/rigging note.
6. **Audit:** completed in `reports/PUBLICATION_AUDIT_I/`.
7. **Decision:** **APPENDIX/EXPOSITORY ONLY**, not an original-paper core.

### RIGGING tradeoff package — DECOMPOSED; NOT ONE THEOREM

1. **Theorem pieces:** evaluation is continuous exactly for (s>1/2); the
   full half-density group is unitary in the inhomogeneous rigging norm only
   at (s=0), while the unit subgroup is unitary for every (s); the one-point
   restriction for (alpha>1/2) has indices ((1,1)).
2. **Status:** the threshold and point interaction are published results;
   covariance and the dual-functional incompatibility are elementary.
3. **Observations only:** no audited source symmetry selects an order, and the
   one-point Green form does not itself generate orbit/Gamma data.
4. **Novelty:** theorem-level pieces are **KNOWN** or
   **KNOWN-IN-DIFFERENT-LANGUAGE**.
5. **Grouping:** background and comparison following the fixed-point lemma.
6. **Audit:** completed in `reports/PUBLICATION_AUDIT_I/`.
7. **Decision:** **EXPOSITORY ONLY**; do not cite the package as one theorem.

### IDENTIFIABILITY.NOGO — APPENDIX-ONLY

1. **Statement:** evenness, real zeros, Paley–Wiener type, and one anchor do
   not identify the Xi limit; an explicit normalized sine family is a
   countermodel.
2. **Proof:** analytic countermodel is self-contained; operator-family clause
   is currently schematic.
3. **Novelty:** elementary unless tied sharply to the CCM claim.
4. **Significance:** useful methodological warning, weak standalone theorem.
5. **Grouping:** appendix in a CCM obstruction note, not the fixed-point paper.
6. **Needed search:** normal-family counterexamples and uniqueness sets for
   sine-type entire functions.
7. **Decision:** **APPENDIX-ONLY**; narrow to analytic statement unless the
   operator counterfamily is written explicitly.

### GLOBAL.CUTOFF.DICHOTOMY — APPENDIX-ONLY

1. **Statement:** harmonic-measure cutoff convergence tolerates off-line
   resonances, while positive-cone convergence to the Weil form implies RH.
2. **Proof:** logically self-contained once Connes and Weil inputs are cited.
3. **Novelty:** known-source synthesis, no novelty claim.
4. **Significance:** clarifies exactly where RH enters; not a new theorem.
5. **Grouping:** conceptual appendix/background.
6. **Needed search:** Connes/Consani statements of global versus semilocal
   positivity.
7. **Decision:** **APPENDIX-ONLY**.

### TAIL.COERCIVITY — KEEP FOR SEPARATE REAUDIT

1. **Statement:** the high Fourier-index block of the fixed-\(\lambda\) Weil
   form is coercive with lower bound tending to infinity.
2. **Proof:** analytic outline is substantial but needs line-by-line checking.
3. **Novelty:** possibly new; unchecked.
4. **Significance:** useful functional-analytic control of CCM finite
   sections, but not directly part of fixed-point rigging obstruction.
5. **Grouping:** possible separate CCM/Galerkin note with Schur lemmas.
6. **Needed search:** coercivity/compact-resolvent proofs in CCM and later
   prolate papers.
7. **Decision:** **KEEP** in research archive; not yet a paper theorem.

### WEIGHTED.SCHUR / LOWRANK.TAIL / exact-resolvent lemmas — APPENDIX-ONLY

1. **Statement:** fixed-\(\lambda=2\), fixed-low-sector tail and finite-rank
   estimates.
2. **Proof:** mixed analytic/Arb chain; not independently reproduced.
3. **Novelty:** unverified.
4. **Significance:** technical support, limited generality.
5. **Grouping:** appendix to a CCM finite-section paper if TAIL.COERCIVITY
   survives.
6. **Needed search:** finite-section Schur complements and CCM matrix decay.
7. **Decision:** **APPENDIX-ONLY**.

### HALF.DENSITY.RIGIDITY — DROP AS NOVEL RESULT

Known in different language in normalized induction and Connes local traces.
Retain as background normalization, not a publication claim.

### DIRECT.L2.DESCENT / CRITICAL.CLOSURE / TRACE.WEIGHT / DUALITY.ONLY — DROP

These are elementary or known-source filters. They are valuable project
guardrails but do not presently supply publishable novelty.

### JET.CUTOFF.ASYMPTOTICS — APPENDIX-ONLY PENDING NOVELTY

The polynomial versus exponential jet calculation is clean, but its relation
to known Mellin truncation/resonance asymptotics must be checked before it is
promoted.

## Bottom line

Publication Audit I removed both fixed-point/rigging items as cores of an
original research paper. They remain useful expository material.
`TAIL.COERCIVITY` is the only separate analytic candidate retained for future
re-audit; everything else is background, appendix-level, or known.
