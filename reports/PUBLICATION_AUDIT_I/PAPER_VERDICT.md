# Paper viability verdict

## Verdict

**VIABLE-ONLY-AS-EXPOSITORY-NOTE.**

The earlier verdict `VIABLE-AFTER-NOVELTY-AUDIT` does not survive as a claim
for an original obstruction paper.  The audit found the analytic spine to be
correct after narrowing, but its substantive pieces are established p-adic
point-interaction theory or elementary Hilbert-space observations.

## Minimal defensible theorem set

An expository note could state and prove:

1. the abstract bounded-graph invisibility lemma;
2. its shrinking-ball application to the finite bounded Tate scaling/Fourier
   source;
3. the abstract non-unimodular fixed-point-character/unitarity lemma;
4. the sharp `H^s(Q_p)` evaluation threshold and scaling covariance, explicitly
   credited as a rederivation of known theory;
5. the known one-point Vladimirov restriction and its Green form;
6. a scoped comparison showing that this one-point form does not itself
   produce `log p`, `+/-m log p`, or the infinity-place Gamma distribution.

Items 1–3 are too elementary, and items 4–5 too known, to support an original
specialist research article without an additional nontrivial theorem.

## Literature work still required before even an expository submission

- MathSciNet and zbMATH forward/backward citation searches from the 2006 and
  2008 Kuzhel–Torba papers.
- Citation search for Posilicano's singular-trace framework and later
  boundary relations on ultrametric spaces.
- Russian-language monographs and papers by Kochubei, Vladimirov, and
  Zúñiga-Galindo on trace/evaluation theorems.
- A focused search for RKHS/unitary-representation statements of the
  non-unimodular evaluation-character lemma.
- Specialist confirmation that no later adelic pseudodifferential model
  canonically fixes the order.
- Exact source citations for the Connes local flat-trace coefficient and the
  separation between point Green form and suspension/orbit measure.

No manuscript should be drafted until the intended venue is explicitly
expository or a genuinely new theorem is added from outside the closed RH
programme.
