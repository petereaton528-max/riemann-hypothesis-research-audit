It is a genuine and correct lemma, but a specialist will recognize it as an
immediate consequence of equivalence between bounded graph norms and `L2`,
plus a shrinking-support test.  The p-adic application is a useful corollary,
not a standalone publication result.

The sharp threshold and extension theory are already published.  The scaling
calculation and abstract unitary/evaluation conflict are elementary.  The
remaining “no canonical order/no arithmetic dynamics” clauses are not strong
enough as universal theorems.

## Likely editorial view

As original research for a specialist operator-theory, p-adic-analysis, or
number-theory journal, the current package is **insufficiently novel**.  It
could support a careful expository or perspective note explaining why a
specific boundary reinterpretation of the Connes/Tate fixed-point trace does
not work.  That note would need to foreground scope and synthesis rather than
claim new point-interaction mathematics.
