# Public audit source manifest

The Publication Audit I directory contains the independent public statements,
proofs, scope tests, literature comparison, significance assessment, and
correction record for the Phase-22/23 candidates.

## Primary public files

- `CORRECTIONS.md`: binding corrections to historical claims.
- `FIXEDPOINT_PRECISE_STATEMENT.md` and `FIXEDPOINT_NEW_PROOF.md`: corrected
  bounded-graph theorem and proof.
- `FIXEDPOINT_SCOPE.md` and `FIXEDPOINT_DEFICIENCY_CONSEQUENCE.md`: exact
  limits of the conclusion.
- `RIGGING_PRECISE_STATEMENT.md`, `POINT_THRESHOLD_PROOF.md`, and
  `SCALING_AUDIT.md`: decomposed p-adic analytic statements.
- `POINT_INTERACTION_LITERATURE.md`: comparison with primary literature.
- `ABSTRACT_GENERALIZATION.md` and `LOGICAL_RELATION.md`: abstract synthesis.
- `NOVELTY.md`, `SIGNIFICANCE.md`, and `PAPER_VERDICT.md`: conservative
  publication assessment.
- `TRADEOFF_DECOMPOSITION.md`: theorem versus observation classification.

The private source proofs and phase narratives are intentionally omitted from
this clean snapshot. Their role is documented in the correction record and
the private provenance commit recorded in `AUTHORITATIVE_FILES.md`.
