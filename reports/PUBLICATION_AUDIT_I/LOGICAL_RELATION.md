# Logical relation of the two candidates

The results are not independent research theorems.

1. `FIXEDPOINT.GRAPH.NOGO` says every topology no stronger than the bounded
   `L2` graph topology misses point evaluation.
2. The Vladimirov scale is the standard repair: for order above `1/2`, its
   unbounded graph topology makes evaluation continuous and creates the known
   rank-one point restriction.
3. The unitary/evaluation lemma then shows that **any** repair retaining the
   original non-unimodular fixed-point character must relinquish full rigging
   unitarity, not merely the Vladimirov repair.
4. The claims about order selection and missing arithmetic orbit data are a
   separate adequacy audit of that repair.

Thus the clean organization is **known obstruction plus known repair plus an
elementary compatibility lemma**.  `RIGGING.TRADEOFF` is best viewed as the
repair-attempt analysis following `FIXEDPOINT.GRAPH`, not as an independent
new theorem.

The two analytic calculations are logically distinct—bounded graph
invisibility does not itself give the `s>1/2` threshold—but they are parts of
one abstract principle: continuous point evaluation requires a genuinely
stronger topology, and its non-unimodular covariance prevents simultaneous
unitarity of the original half-density action.

For a paper, the arithmetic comparison should follow these analytic lemmas as
an application/discussion section.  It must not be promoted into a universal
no-go for all arithmetic boundary carriers.
