# Riemann Hypothesis Research Audit

> **RIEMANN HYPOTHESIS REMAINS OPEN.**

This repository does **not** claim:

- a proof or disproof of the Riemann hypothesis (RH);
- impossibility of any audited approach outside its explicitly stated natural
  class;
- publication-level novelty for results classified as known, known in
  different language, project corollaries, or novelty-unverified;
- that numerical or heuristic evidence is a theorem.

This is a technical research audit, negative-results and route-map archive,
and record of explored spectral and non-spectral approaches to RH. Its purpose
is to reduce duplicated work, separate realization of zeta zeros from actual
critical-line forcing, and state precisely why several natural constructions
do not achieve their intended goal.

The private research project was consolidated through Phase 26 and then
subjected to an independent proof and novelty re-audit of its two leading
publication candidates. That re-audit narrowed one result and decomposed the
other into known analytic facts and architectural observations. This public
snapshot incorporates those corrections.

## What is included

- a conceptual public report;
- a master route map and exact closure scopes;
- narrow open problems and research debt;
- the corrected fixed-point bounded-graph corollary;
- the p-adic point-evaluation, scaling, and point-interaction audit;
- conservative publication and novelty assessments;
- selected proofs only, with explicit status headers;
- citations and links, but no redistributed journal articles or books.

Large numerical artifacts, private checkpoints, speculative proof attempts,
downloaded papers, caches, and machine-specific material are deliberately
excluded.

## Core conclusion

The project found and reconstructed genuine arithmetic trace and zero-bearing
structures, especially in the Tate/Connes/Meyer/CCM setting. It did not find
an independent positivity, self-adjointness, purity, polarization, or
zero-confinement mechanism that proves RH. Broad exploratory RH work is
therefore paused.
