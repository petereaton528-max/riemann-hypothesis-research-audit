# Locked Riemann trace fingerprint

Any proposed carrier discussed in this archive was tested against the same
source-derived requirements:

1. average nontrivial-zero density of order `(T/2pi)log(T/2pi)`;
2. the exact Archimedean Gamma/digamma distribution;
3. finite-place support at `+/-m log p`, `m>=1`;
4. finite-place coefficient `(log p)p^{-m/2}`;
5. local-to-global compatibility and the functional equation;
6. multiplicity-sensitive nontrivial-zero data;
7. an independent reality, positivity, purity, or stability mechanism not
   defined from the zero set and not merely equivalent to RH.

Matching only prime lengths, only a Gamma factor, or only a zero set is not
sufficient. This locked target explains why many attractive analogies were
classified as incomplete or closed in a natural class.
