# Material excluded from the public snapshot

The following private-project categories were intentionally not copied:

- Git history and private checkpoints;
- downloaded papers, books, PDFs, and screenshots;
- large numerical tables, interval-arithmetic dumps, logs, and generated
  artifacts;
- speculative or failed proof attempts without continuing audit value;
- machine-specific paths, caches, local configuration, and authentication
  material;
- scripts whose correctness or licensing was not independently reviewed;
- duplicated phase narratives superseded by consolidation or Publication
  Audit I.

Excluded third-party literature is represented by citations and public links
in `REFERENCES.md`. Exclusion is not a judgment on the cited work; it is a
redistribution and repository-hygiene decision.
